COMMAND REFERENCE

Left click / dragSelect ship(s). Shift adds to selection.
Right click seaMove there (selected keep formation). Shift queues waypoints.
Right click enemyDesignate target for guns.
F, then click enemyLaunch an anti-ship missile salvo. Z/X/C sets salvo size 2/4/6.
GFire chaff decoys — use when VAMPIRES are inbound.
BRadar ON / OFF. On: each ping finds contacts out to your range and your SAMs work — but enemies near you can see you radiating. Off: hidden, but blind and no SAMs.
J, then clickCast a jamming cone. Enemy radars, SAMs and missile seekers inside it are blinded for a few seconds. Recharges after use.
Amber blipsUnknown radar contacts. They might be warships or civilian freighters — get closer, or use a fire-control radar, to identify. Missiles hit the first ship near their aim point.
PActive sonar ping: finds submarines nearby exactly — but every enemy within twice the range hears it.
USubmarine: dive / surface. Submerged subs are invisible to radar; only sonar finds them. Use torpedoes against them.
DetectionThe fleets start out of radar range. Small hulls are harder for radar to see than big ones. A radar or jammer that is switched on can be heard from farther away than it can see, but that gives only a rough, drifting position.
IslandsLand blocks radar, sight and sea-skimming missiles. A ship within 350 m of a coast hides in the radar clutter: enemy radar sees it at only half range (card shows COAST MASK).
TrafficFreighters come and go from every direction; many follow a hidden shipping lane that runs through the control point. Airliners cross high overhead (air defences ignore them). Whales show up on passive sonar like submarines; an active ping tells them apart.
I / O / K / LCall a squadron from your air wing (designed in the fleet creator). Click a patrol point, or an identified enemy ship if it carries anti-ship missiles. It flies in from your edge, works for a while, then goes home.
AircraftHelicopters and drones fly from ships with a hangar. Select and move them like ships. SAMs, CIWS and lasers shoot them down; flares halve the chance.
Control pointHold the ringed area alone to capture it. The holder scores; first to 300 wins. Sinking the enemy fleet also wins.
[ / ] or R / VRing up more / less speed. Backspace = all stop.
1–4, `Select ship / whole fleet. Double-tap to jump the camera.
H / double-clickFollow selected ship. M = ride the newest missile.
WASD / arrows, Q / EPan, rotate. Middle-drag or right-drag also rotates.
Space, TPause, time compression (vs AI only).

Your SAMs and CIWS shoot down incoming missiles automatically. Big salvos saturate defences — small ones get swatted.
